# Audio

## Music
- `Music/FantasyTombScene.ogg` - "Fantasy Tomb Scene OST" by Markus Schroeder (http://markus-projects.net/),
  from https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-tomb-scene-ost, licensed CC-BY-SA 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Used unchanged.

## Sound effects
- Everything in `Sfx/` was synthesised by the team with a script (sine, square and filtered noise): no samples
  and no third-party material. Replace any file to change a sound; the slots are in `Resources/AudioConfig`.
